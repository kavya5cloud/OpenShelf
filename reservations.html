How reservations work

  1. Reserve any book from the catalog.
  2. If a copy is free, it's set aside for you right away.
  3. If all copies are issued, you join a queue — first come, first served.
  4. When it's your turn, you get a notification.
  5. Collect it within 2 days, or it goes to the next person.
